🛡️ Your Opponent's Move Filters

These settings determine which of your opponent's moves BookBuilder will include in your repertoire.
BookBuilder will calculate the likelihood of your opponent's move being played in your games. Increasing the second number here will therefore increase the size of your repertoire. For example, if you select 1 in 500 games, BookBuilder will prepare a repertoire with all your opponent's replies that are likely to occur more than once per 500 games you play, according to the data from your Lichess Database settings. This 1 in X games number assumes you are always playing with white or black. It includes the probability of reaching the starting position of your repertoire, even if that position is fairly deep (eg Najdorf). BookBuilder assumes that the moves you play are always chosen and so do not affect the probability of reaching a position.
Moves in the Lichess Database played less times than this will not be included in your repertoire. You don't want to be generating likely lines from tiny amounts of games.

The smaller this number the more likely BookBuilder will select moves with better winrates but less data. Technically, this is the confidence interval alpha (EG 95% CI) for deciding the lower bounds of how good a move's winrate is.
By default, draws count as half a point for winrate calculations. But you may want a 'must win' repertoire, where a draw is as good as a loss. If so, untick this.
